The problem

Enterprise decisions depend on fragmented evidence.

Critical facts are divided across databases, business applications, spreadsheets, policies, contracts, and operational documents. Existing assistants usually see only one source at a time.

01

Disconnected context

ERP, HR, payroll, insurance, CRM, files, and APIs each contain only part of the business story.

02

Inconsistent answers

Search tools and analytics tools operate separately, making cross-source investigation slow and manual.

03

Enterprise trust gap

Regulated organizations require permissions, citations, audit trails, private deployment, and human approval.

The solution

One governed intelligence layer across enterprise sources.

Data Lense selects only the capabilities needed for each question and reconciles the resulting evidence into one transparent answer.

STEP 1

Understand

Interpret the business question and permitted scope.

STEP 2

Plan

Select structured data, enterprise knowledge, web, or approved extensions.

STEP 3

Investigate

Query live systems or search permission-aware indexed content.

STEP 4

Validate

Verify calculations, citations, dates, authority, and source conflicts.

STEP 5

Respond

Present facts, inferences, limitations, and governed next actions.

Example investigation
“Which rejected insurance claims may conflict with the policy that applied at the time?”
✓
Review claim records
Status, rejection reason, value, customer, and event date.
✓
Search the applicable policy
Coverage rules, exclusions, authority, and effective dates.
✓
Reconcile the evidence
Match each claim to the correct policy version and expose conflicts.
✓
Keep people in control
Recommend review and generate an approval request instead of acting autonomously.
